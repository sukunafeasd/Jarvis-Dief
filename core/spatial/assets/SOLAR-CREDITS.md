# Planet textures

Solar System Scope / INOVE, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Local *-sss.json contains the downloaded image, resized to 1024x512 and JPEG
encoded at quality 80, in a data URI. Colors, lighting and scale in the scene
are artistic; the maps contain reconstructed areas and are not live imagery.
Originals: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/download/2k_NAME.jpg
NAME: mercury, venus_atmosphere, mars, jupiter, saturn, uranus, neptune,
sun, earth_clouds. Earth and Moon also use Solar System Scope maps (earth_daymap, moon):
the earlier NASA file paths were absent locally and have been replaced.

Planet facts: NASA Science pages linked in planet-facts.mjs. Values are
rounded; planetary diameters are equatorial where indicated. Artistic
orbits do not represent real-time ephemerides or measured distances.
